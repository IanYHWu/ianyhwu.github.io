Hello! I am a second year PhD student at Carnegie Mellon's [Machine Learning Department](https://www.ml.cmu.edu/). I am advised by [Aviral Kumar](https://aviralkumar2907.github.io/).

Before coming to CMU, I studied chemistry at Cambridge and machine learning at UCL, where I worked on deep reinforcement learning at [UCL DARK](https://ucldark.com/). I also spent a few years working at [C3 AI](https://c3.ai/), where I trained LLMs to code in proprietary languages. 

## Research

My research centres on exploring and optimising the ability of LLMs to <span style="color: #1ABC9C;">**learn, reason and adapt at test time**</span>: how can we best *train* models to utilise lots and lots of test-time compute to solve very complex and very long-horizon reasoning tasks?


## Other Interests

I was a competitive debater in my younger years, and represented both Hong Kong and the Cambridge Union at various competitions, including the European Universities Debating Championships, which I won in 2019. These days, I enjoy playing the piano (Beethoven, Chopin etc.), playing bullet chess, and finding creative ways to win at Civilization VI.
